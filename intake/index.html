Guest check in

Send us your details before the session.

Every session is confirmed the same way. Fill this in once and the studio has what it needs to open the door, set the room up and give you the address. It takes about three minutes.

How it works

  1. You send this form. Your details, who is coming with you, your ID photo, your hours and what you are booking.
  2. The studio confirms. You get a reply with the address, the door code and the total for your hours at 45 dollars an hour.
  3. You put a card on file. The studio sends you a secure link from its payment processor. Nothing is charged for the booking itself and no money is frozen.
  4. You show the ID at the door. The name on it has to match the name on this form. Everyone you bring does the same.

Have these ready

  • Your ID. A photo of the front of your driver license or state ID. The name has to match the name you put on the form.
  • First and last name of everyone you are bringing. Artists, engineers, friends, anyone walking in. Each adult brings their own ID.
  • Your date and start time, and roughly how many hours you want.
  • What you are booking for. Recording, podcast, video, mixing, mastering, photos, or a mix of them.

Guests who are not on this form do not get in. That is not about you. It is how the room stays insurable and how everyone keeps track of their own gear.

The card on file

The studio keeps a card on file for every session. Here is exactly what that means.

  • Nothing is frozen. No deposit is taken and no money is held off your balance. Your available funds do not change.
  • You enter it on the payment processor secure page, not here and not over email. The studio never sees or stores your full card number.
  • It is only charged for damage, missing equipment, or time past the hours you booked. If none of that happens, it is never charged.
  • Session time is paid separately, the normal way, at 45 dollars an hour.

Never send a card number by email, by text, or through this form. Anyone who asks you to is not the studio. The only place a card goes is the secure link the studio sends you after your booking is confirmed.
