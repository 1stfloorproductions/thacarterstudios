Every session is confirmed the same way. Fill this in once and the studio has what it needs to open the door, set the room up and give you the address. It takes about three minutes.

How it works

  1. You fill this in. It opens an email to the studio with all your answers already written out.
  2. You attach a photo of your ID to that email, then send it. The studio cannot confirm a session without it.
  3. The studio replies with the address, the door code and the total for your hours at 45 dollars an hour.
  4. You show the same ID at the door. The name has to match. Everyone you bring does the same.

The card on file

The studio keeps a card on file for every session. Here is exactly what that means.

  • Nothing is frozen. No deposit is taken and no money is held off your balance. Your available funds do not change.
  • It never goes through this form. The studio sends you a secure link afterwards and you enter it there. The studio never sees or stores your full card number.
  • It is only charged for damage, missing equipment, or time past the hours you booked. If none of that happens, it is never charged.
  • Session time is paid separately, the normal way, at 45 dollars an hour.

Never put a card number in this form, in an email, or in a text. Anyone who asks you to is not the studio. The only place a card goes is the secure link the studio sends you after your booking is confirmed.
